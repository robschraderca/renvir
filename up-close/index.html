03 · The idea

Their room is a white lab. Ours is black glass at six in the morning.

Wet black slate, black acrylic, one hard tungsten light from the side, water everywhere. The three black bottles are the only objects. The only color in the film is what comes out of them: the gray of Alpha Detox™ turning to white foam, the glow of Citrus Revive™ with light behind it, the white of Supercharger™ holding its peak. Where Vichy uses green, we use the liquid and the light. Carbon and tungsten, nothing else in the room.

The man is in it and never seen. Two hands, a jaw, a neck under running water, a towel on a shoulder. The Vichy reel turns on a smile to camera. Ours turns on a hand wiping steam off the mirror and the jaw in the glass, done for the day. Then the three stand in a line.

One sound per cut. The drop ticks, the pump clicks, the lid twists, water hits slate. Cut on the sound, not the picture. A low slow track underneath, closer to an engine at idle than a spa. No voice in the first version. A second version carries four lines in my voice for the people who watch with sound on.

08 · How we shoot it

This is the one film where the real thing is cheaper and better than generation. Generators get labels wrong, and they get the way a real drop bends light wrong. So the plan is practical first, with generation only as the fallback for the one abstract shot.

The table

A sheet of black acrylic and a black slate tile. One hard light from the side with a snoot, black foam core to kill the spill. A clear glass dish for the light through the serum shots. A spray bottle and a dropper. Glycerin and water so the beads hold. A tripod.

Camera: iPhone at 4K 120 fps with a clip-on macro lens, or a mirrorless body with a 100mm macro. Either works at this distance. The whole kit is under two hundred dollars, one time, and it is inside the fee.

The product and the hands

Two full sets. One set stays dry and perfect for the heroes and the lineup. One set gets pumped, wet and used. Labels checked letter by letter before anything is cut.

Real hands, no actor. Trimmed nails, no watch, no ring. Shot from the man's point of view or from the side so we see a jaw and a neck and nothing above.

If a plate has to be generated

Only the abstract shots with no label and no skin in frame, which on this film means the rising bubble macro and nothing else. Live per run price stated before each roll, one roll per prompt, stop after three misses and rethink. Any jar motion is Kling 3.0 Pro with the jar layer frozen from the still. Still plates are Seedream 4. Skin contact is Veo 3.1 only and never in the same frame as the jar or the marks.

Marks, type, safe zones

Renvir Wordmark White with the registered R, top left from frame one, never fades. TM on Alpha Detox, Citrus Revive, Supercharger and Never Stop. Oswald for supers, Inter for the small line. Everything kept out of the top 14 percent and the bottom third on 9:16, nothing below the footer. The 4:5 feed cut comes from the same frames with the supers moved up.

On black versus white. Vichy is a lab, so white is their truth. White would make us read as a pharmacy brand and would fight every piece we have shipped. Black glass with tungsten keeps the same macro grammar and stays ours. If you want to see both, a white tile version is a second setup on the same day, same shots, about forty five minutes, and a cheap test.
