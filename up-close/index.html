Concept · October 2026

Up close

Our version of the Vichy reel. Macro, slow motion, one sound per cut, no face. Their room is a white lab. Ours is black glass. The three bottles are the only things in frame and the only color is what comes out of them.

Why this one

  • The bottles look expensive. Light, water and a macro lens on the real jar. That is the answer to the bottles in the last ad.
  • It is the Oct 8 positioning. Ingredients, premium, black and white, and it ends on the complete system.
  • It needs no face. Hands, a jaw, a neck. The smile at the end becomes a wiped mirror.

The film · 26 seconds

Hook0 to 3 sec

One drop of Citrus Revive™ leaves the pump in slow motion, black field, light behind it. It lands in a pool of serum on black glass. Wordmark in top left, stays.

Alpha Detox™3 to 11 sec

Hero: the black bottle on wet slate, water running down it, slow push. Gray cleanser meets water and goes to foam. Two hands work it up the jaw. Water takes it off in one sheet. Super: Charcoal. Willow bark. Rosemary.

Citrus Revive™11 to 17 sec

Hero: the bottle beaded with water. Light through the serum, bubbles rising. Two pumps, fingertips press it into the cheek, the sheen catches the light. Super: Vitamin C. Citrus stem cells.

Supercharger™17 to 21 sec

Hero: the lid twists off, one highlight on the cream. Two fingers take a swipe, it holds its peak, spreads white to clear on skin. Super: Hyaluronic acid. Vitamin C. Chamomile.

The turn21 to 23 sec

Lid clicks back on. A hand wipes steam off the mirror. The jaw and a towel on the shoulder. Not the eyes.

Lineup23 to 26 sec

The three on black glass, beaded, slow push. NEVER STOP.™ LOOKING YOUNGER. Then RENVIR® RENEWED MAN. Then renvirco.com.

Sound: no voice. Foley on every cut (drop, pump, foam, water, lid) over a low slow track. A four line VO version in my voice if we want it. Cutdowns: one 10 second piece per product from the same frames, plus the 4:5.

How it gets built

  • The jar is the real jar. Packshot still, water beads added, slow pushes in Kling 3.0 Pro with the jar layer frozen.
  • Liquid and texture plates generated. The drop, the bubbles, the foam, the cream. No label in those frames. Seedream 4 stills, Kling 3.0 Pro for motion.
  • Hands and jaw in Veo 3.1, never in the same frame as the jar or the marks. Composited in After Effects.
  • Words on screen are ingredient names only until we have the list of which claims are substantiated per product.
