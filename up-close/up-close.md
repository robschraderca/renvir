# Renvir: Up Close

Short version, October 9, 2026. Companion to `up-close/index.html`.

Reference: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdZcX0dM-Qz/ (Jaiden Wood for Vichy Dercos). 26 seconds, 23 shots, no voiceover, foley synced to every cut, one face at 22 seconds, lineup of three at the end.

## The idea

Our version of the Vichy reel. Macro, slow motion, one sound per cut, no face. Their room is a white lab, ours is black glass. The three bottles are the only objects and the only color is what comes out of them.

## Why this one

- The bottles look expensive. Light, water and a macro lens on the real jar. That answers Alex's note on the bottles in the last ad.
- It is the Oct 8 positioning: ingredients, premium, black and white, ends on the complete system.
- It needs no face. Hands, a jaw, a neck. The smile at the end becomes a wiped mirror.

## The film, 26 seconds

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 | One drop of Citrus Revive leaves the pump in slow motion, black field, light behind it. Lands in a pool of serum on black glass. Wordmark top left, stays. |
| Alpha Detox | 3 to 11 | Hero: black bottle on wet slate, water running down it, slow push. Gray cleanser meets water, goes to foam. Two hands work it up the jaw. Water takes it off in one sheet. Super: Charcoal. Willow bark. Rosemary. |
| Citrus Revive | 11 to 17 | Hero: bottle beaded with water. Light through the serum, bubbles rising. Two pumps, fingertips press it into the cheek, the sheen catches the light. Super: Vitamin C. Citrus stem cells. |
| Supercharger | 17 to 21 | Hero: lid twists off, one highlight on the cream. Two fingers take a swipe, it holds its peak, spreads white to clear on skin. Super: Hyaluronic acid. Vitamin C. Chamomile. |
| The turn | 21 to 23 | Lid clicks back on. A hand wipes steam off the mirror. The jaw and a towel on the shoulder. Not the eyes. |
| Lineup | 23 to 26 | The three on black glass, beaded, slow push. NEVER STOP. LOOKING YOUNGER. / RENVIR RENEWED MAN. / renvirco.com |

Sound: no voice. Foley on every cut (drop, pump, foam, water, lid) over a low slow track. Optional four line VO version in Rob's voice. Cutdowns: one 10 second piece per product from the same frames, plus the 4:5.

## How it gets built

- The jar is the real jar: packshot still, water beads added, slow pushes in Kling 3.0 Pro with the jar layer frozen.
- Liquid and texture plates generated: drop, bubbles, foam, cream. No label in those frames. Seedream 4 stills, Kling 3.0 Pro for motion.
- Hands and jaw in Veo 3.1, never in the same frame as the jar or the marks. Composited in After Effects.
- Paid runs follow the standing rule: live per run price stated first, one roll per prompt, stop after three misses.
- Words on screen are ingredient names only until the list of substantiated claims per product exists.
