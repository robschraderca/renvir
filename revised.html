How success gets measured

Each line gets judged on what it actually controls, with numbers, in writing, every week. Revenue, conversions and return on spend live in Ray's lane and his dashboards. These two lines get measured upstream of that, on the things that make his numbers possible.

Line one succeeds when the creative works

Hook rate: the share of people who stay past the first three seconds, the most sensitive read on whether a piece stops the scroll. Hold rate: whether they stay through it. Usage rate: what share of delivered pieces Ray actually puts live, because creative he does not run is creative that missed. Winners found: pieces that earn scale each month, with the replacement built before the current one fatigues. If hook rates climb and Ray is never waiting on creative, line one is doing its job.

Line two succeeds when the account comes alive

Non follower reach: is the content escaping the existing audience, the truest organic growth signal. Saves and shares: the signals Meta rewards with distribution. Profile visits and link taps: attention turning into site sessions. Response time: every comment and DM answered inside a day. Organic winners promoted to paid: the lab's output, counted monthly. Follower growth: tracked every week against the baseline trend, with the 60 and 90 day targets we set together after month one.
