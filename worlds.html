RENVIR  /  THE WORLDS

One man. Many rooms.

Three quick concepts, built in one night, to show the direction. The man never changes. The room does. Everything here is a rough cut. The finished pieces get tuned, graded and cleaned up properly.

01

The Truck

REEL  /  16 SEC  /  ROUGH CUT WITH VOICE

He's driven it hard. Morning comes anyway. Two fingers of Supercharger, worked in, and he faces the day. This is the full shape of a product reel: the world, the man, the jar, the routine, the sign off.

Scratch voice and temp music. The real jar is the real jar. The end panel is the layout we would carry across every product.

02

The Wall

OPENER  /  8 SEC  /  NO SOUND YET

Two thirds of the way up El Capitan. Nothing under him but the valley. He drives up through one foot, pulls, and reaches for the next hold. This is the opening shot of the second world. The sink and the sign off follow the same way as the truck.

Picture only. Copy and voice to come once the line is chosen.

03

The Paddock

OPENER  /  5 SEC  /  NO SOUND YET

Race day. The suit, the car in Renvir livery, the crew behind him. He looks the car over, then turns to the stands. Same man, a third room. Racing is one of the rooms, not the whole house.

Picture only. The livery, the patch and the helmet get locked properly in the finished version.
